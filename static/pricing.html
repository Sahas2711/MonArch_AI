Frequently Asked Questions

What is the 45-day payment rule under the MSME Act?
Section 15 of the MSME Development Act 2006 mandates that any buyer who purchases goods or services from a registered MSME must make payment within the period agreed upon in writing, which cannot exceed 45 days under any circumstances. If no written agreement exists, payment is legally due within 15 days.
Can a buyer contractually override the 45-day rule?
No. Section 15 has overriding effect. Any clause in a purchase order or master agreement specifying 60, 90, or 120 days is legally void ab initio under Indian law.
How does Section 43B(h) of the Income Tax Act help MSMEs?
Introduced by Finance Act 2023, Section 43B(h) disallows deductions for buyer companies for any sum payable to Micro and Small enterprises if unpaid beyond the Section 15 timeline (45/15 days). This means buyers face high additional income tax on unpaid invoices, creating massive leverage for suppliers to get paid promptly.
